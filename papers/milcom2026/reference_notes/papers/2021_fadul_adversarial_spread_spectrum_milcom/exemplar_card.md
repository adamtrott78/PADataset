This paper is included because it is a MILCOM RF/security paper with adversarial communications framing, a bounded threat model, and concrete contribution bullets. It is useful as a venue-style reference, but it is not the strongest writing exemplar in the library.

## 7. Do Emulate

- Use concrete contribution bullets.
- Define adversary/system assumptions explicitly.
- Keep the RF/security problem connected to tactical constraints.

## 8. Do Not Emulate

- Do not open with generic statements about deep learning succeeding in image and NLP domains.
- Do not let background consume the first-page momentum.
- Do not over-list contributions if one central story can carry the paper.

## 9. Extracted Heuristics

- MILCOM reviewers accept adversarial RF framing when the operational scenario is bounded.
- A threat model can be short and useful when it names actor capabilities.
- Our introduction should start closer to the RF/EMS failure mode than this paper does.

## 10. Decision

Decision: STYLE_REFERENCE

Rationale: Useful MILCOM RF/security reference, but weaker than Searchlight, Stitching, HyperAdv, SpecForce, and Stealth as a writing model.
