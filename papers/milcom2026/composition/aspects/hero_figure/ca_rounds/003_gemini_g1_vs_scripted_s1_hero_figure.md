# Comparative Analysis Round 003: Gemini G1 vs. Scripted S1 Hero Figure

## Aspect

hero_figure

## Purpose

This round compares the first Gemini-generated candidate, G1, against the first deterministic SVG candidate, S1. The goal is to decide whether the hero figure should continue through image-generation refinement or move to a scripted vector workflow.

## Inputs

- Gemini G1: generated from `hero_figure_visual_spec_v001.md`.
- Scripted S1: generated by `generate_hero_figure_s1.py`.

## Result

The scripted SVG/Inkscape workflow wins as the long-term production path.

Gemini G1 was useful for confirming the overall visual concept, but it is not suitable as the final source of a publication figure because it is not deterministic, not easily editable, and may render notation/text inconsistently. S1 has fixable layout issues, but it is reproducible and can be improved systematically.

## Gemini G1 strengths

1. It understood the four-region structure:
   - OTA RF Observation
   - PA Backbone
   - DQNGuard
   - Outputs and Downstream Use

2. It produced a reasonably compact left-to-right academic block diagram.

3. It validated that the visual specification was coherent enough for a generative model to interpret.

## Gemini G1 weaknesses

1. Raster-style generation is not safely editable.
2. Font sizes and mathematical notation are not fully controllable.
3. Future revisions would require repeated prompt attempts instead of deterministic changes.
4. It cannot serve as a reproducible figure artifact in the CA pipeline.

## Scripted S1 strengths

1. It is deterministic and reproducible.
2. It produces SVG/PDF outputs suitable for LaTeX inclusion.
3. It gives exact control over dimensions, positions, colors, line widths, and labels.
4. It fits the CA system: visual spec -> script -> artifact -> QA -> revision.

## Scripted S1 weaknesses

1. Arrowheads are too large and visually distracting.
2. Some arrows appear directionally ambiguous or visually reversed.
3. Backbone fan-out connectors are too loud.
4. The input panel wastes vertical space.
5. The output/downstream region is cramped.
6. The bottom note crowds the downstream modules and should be removed.
7. Text is readable but slightly oversized for an IEEE double-column figure.

## S2 revision plan

Candidate S2 should keep the S1 concept but revise the rendering:

1. Replace SVG marker-based arrows with custom drawn arrowheads.
2. Shrink arrowheads substantially.
3. Reduce text sizes by roughly 10 to 15 percent.
4. Shorten the canvas height.
5. Remove the bottom note.
6. Simplify PA Backbone connectors.
7. Make the DQNGuard box slightly less tall and more balanced.
8. Make outputs/downstream boxes cleaner and more evenly spaced.
9. Keep DQNGuard as the visual center.
10. Preserve the four-region evidence-flow structure.

## Decision

Proceed with the scripted vector workflow.

Next candidate: Scripted S2.
